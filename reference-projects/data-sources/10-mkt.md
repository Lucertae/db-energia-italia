## Provider (da README Data Sources) (12)

1. Coinbase Advanced Trade WebSocket — real-time crypto, L2 book (no auth)
2. Coinbase Exchange REST — historical candles, order-book snapshot (no auth)
3. Yahoo Finance REST — stocks, macro, options, earnings (no auth)
4. FRED REST CSV — economic series via FRED: prefix (no auth)
5. DeFiLlama REST — per-chain TVL (no auth)
6. Binance Futures REST — funding rate + open interest BTC/ETH/SOL (no auth)
7. Yahoo Finance RSS — news headlines (no auth)
8. MarketWatch RSS — news headlines (no auth)
9. CNBC RSS — news headlines (no auth)
10. SEC EDGAR Atom RSS — per-ticker filings 8-K, 10-Q, 10-K (no auth)
11. ntfy.sh — alert push (optional, no signup)
12. Pushover — alert push (optional, free dev)
